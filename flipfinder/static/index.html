    Item sets

    A Grand Exchange clerk swaps a set for its pieces, or the pieces for the set, for free. When the market prices the two differently you can trade the gap. Unpack buys the set and sells each piece. Pack buys every piece and sells the set. Each piece pays its own tax. Click a row to see the pieces.

    Profit assumes patient offers: buying at the instant-sell price and selling at the instant-buy price, with every offer filling. Instant is what crossing the spread on every item pays right now. It is usually negative, and when it is positive the gap is there for the taking. Sets trade thinly, so check the age. Every set is listed; the ones that would not make money right now are dimmed.

    Magic

    Buy an item and cast on it. Alchemy turns it into coins, which pay no GE tax. Enchanting turns it into something worth more, which you then sell. Profit is per item, after the runes and any tax. Alchemy only lists items that can pay; every enchant is listed, with the ones that would not make money right now dimmed.

    Items per 4h is capped three ways: the buy limit, your share of the volume, and how many casts fit in four hours. Bolt spells enchant ten bolts a cast. Elemental runes count as free by default, since a staff or tome supplies them; switch that off if you buy them.
